        About this guide

        Names, descriptions and grades are the official PlayStation 5 list as published on Exophase and PSNProfiles (55 trophies: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze, 28 hidden). Every guide is written for this entry, in our own words, and cites its sources beside it. Where the official description does not name a place or a step, the guide says so instead of inventing one.

        This guide works offline: every file it loads lives in its own folder, and it makes no network request. It shows no Rockstar logo or official artwork.
